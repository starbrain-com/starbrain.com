About

StarBrain

StarBrain is a Netherlands-based engineering consultancy founded by Erik Stabij, bringing 25+ years of production software experience — from embedded systems and real-time C++ to cloud-scale Python, ML pipelines, and data platforms. MSc in Physics & Computer Science from Leiden University, with a neural networks thesis.

We've delivered enterprise RAG systems, high-throughput data pipelines processing millions of events, ML-driven optimisation engines, and complex microservice architectures — for companies ranging from logistics and retail to fintech and media.

StarBrain operates through StarBrain Media BV. We typically embed in engineering teams that need depth, speed, and people who've done it before.
